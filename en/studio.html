The studio

Eddy Illustration represents illustrators and animators.

About

Eddy Illustration is an illustration and animation studio in Paris. We represent illustrators and animators and we produce projects with them: casting, art direction, illustration, 2D and 3D animation.

The group

Eddy Illustration is the illustration and animation house of the Eddy group, a production company based in Paris.

Approach

We define with you a visual narrative and an identity in line with the brand's values and purpose, then we choose the artist and we produce the images.

Services
  • CastingArtist research, briefing and contracting.
  • Art directionVisual development, moodboards and look creation.
  • Illustration and designCustom artwork for branding, packaging and editorial.
  • 2D and 3D animationCharacters, motion graphics and immersive content.
