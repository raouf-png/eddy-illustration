The agency05

Eddy Illustration

Eddy Illustration is the illustrators' agency of the Eddy group, a production company in Paris. We represent artists with a distinct style and, when the project calls for it, we carry it from the still image to animation and film.

  • The right artistWe read your brief and propose the illustrator whose style fits it.
  • From print to filmOne image carried across publishing, campaigns, animation and film by the group's teams.
  • One point of contactQuotes, contracts, rights and delivery.
